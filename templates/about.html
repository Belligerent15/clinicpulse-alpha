About ClinicPulse

Know before you go. Empowering the Sekhukhune community with real-time health information.

🌍

Why this exists

Residents often travel long distances to clinics without knowing their status, only to find long queues or closed doors. ClinicPulse solves this by letting the community update live statuses (Open/Closed, Queue Length, Medicine Availability) right from their phones.

⚠️ Alpha Release

This is an alpha release. All clinic details, locations, phone numbers and status indicators are sample / unverified data and must be confirmed with the clinic before use.

🚑 Medical Disclaimer

ClinicPulse does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call the South African emergency number 10177 or go to the nearest hospital immediately.
