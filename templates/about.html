About ClinicPulse

Know before you go.

ClinicPulse is a mobile-first, low-data web app that helps residents of Sekhukhune District in Limpopo find nearby clinics, see which services are available, check whether the clinic is open, how long the queue is, and whether chronic medication is in stock.

Why this exists

Residents often travel long distances to clinics without knowing their status. ClinicPulse makes a quick check possible before leaving home.

Alpha disclaimer

This is an alpha release. All clinic details, locations, phone numbers and status indicators are sample / unverified data and must be confirmed with the clinic before use.

Medical disclaimer

ClinicPulse does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. In an emergency, call the South African emergency number 10177 or go to the nearest hospital.

Reporting problems

If you spot wrong information on a clinic page, please use the “Report incorrect information” button on that clinic.
